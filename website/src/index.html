What it does

A biographer, an archive, and a voice, all on one computer.

Guided life-story interview

Chapter by chapter, by voice or typing: childhood, work, love, the hard years. Answers become memory cards you read, fix and approve before the twin uses them.

Never invents your life

The twin only answers from memories you approved, cites where each answer came from, and removes any claim it can't back up. Ask what it was never told and it says so.

Your voice, locally

Record a few samples and the twin can reply in a voice like yours, in about 2–4 seconds. Speech recognition runs locally too, so you can just talk to it.

Bring in a lifetime

Import photos with captions, letters, and old message exports. Each one becomes a memory waiting for your review, never published on its own.

Family and heirs

Name the people you trust. They get their own access to sit with your twin, now or later. Seal letters that open on a date, an age, or the day you choose.

A helpful everyday assistant

Reminders at the right local time, notes, search across your own files, private web search, and calendar and email that only read and draft. It never sends anything for you. Every skill is off until you turn it on.

Built for whoever buys it

It picks the best brain your computer can run.

On first run, Heirloom checks your graphics card and installs the open model that scored best on our honesty and chat tests for that hardware. Nothing to configure.

  • Open models only, run through Ollama on your own machine. Heirloom refuses to switch to a cloud model.
  • Tested, not guessed: we benchmarked 20 open models on grounding, chat, tool use and speed before choosing.
  • Works without a gaming card too. It's slower, but just as careful.

How it works

Four steps, about an evening to start.

No accounts to make with us, no subscriptions to an AI company, and no terminal.

Installation questions

  1. Install

    Run one Windows installer and tick "Run Heirloom fully on this PC." It needs no admin rights, no WSL and no reboot, and it sets up the model, voice and speech recognition for your hardware.

  2. Tell your story

    Sit with the interviewer for a chapter at a time, or import photos, letters and messages.

  3. Review what it keeps

    Every memory is a card you can read, edit or discard. The twin only uses what you've approved.

  4. Share with family

    Name heirs, pair phones by QR code, and let family talk with the twin, now or one day later.

Private by design

Your life story shouldn't live on someone else's server.

Questions

The short answers

Does anything I tell it go to the cloud?

No. The AI model, voice, speech recognition and your archive run on your own PC or Twin Box. The only things that use the internet are ones you switch on yourself: private web search, connecting your calendar or email, and the optional phone line.

What happens if it doesn't know something?

It says so, something like "I don't have that memory yet." In our test suite every model size declined all 21 questions about things it was never told.

Do I need a gaming PC?

No. A recent NVIDIA or AMD graphics card makes it faster, but Heirloom also runs on PCs without one (16 GB of RAM or more), just more slowly.

Can I clone someone else's voice?

Only with their clear permission. Heirloom is built to preserve your own voice, or a family member's who has agreed to take part.
