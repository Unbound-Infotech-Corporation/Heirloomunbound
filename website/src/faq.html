FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Privacy and honesty

Does anything I tell Heirloom go to the cloud?

No. The AI model, voice, speech recognition and your archive run on your own PC or Twin Box. Only features you switch on yourself use the internet: private web search, connecting calendar or email, and the optional phone line. The full list.

What happens if the twin doesn't know something?

It says so. Questions about your life are answered only from memories you approved, with a note of where the answer came from, and any claim your memories don't support is removed. In our test suite every model size declined all 21 questions about things it was never told.

Can I see and change what it remembers?

Yes. Every memory is a card you can read, edit or delete. Imports and interview answers wait for your review before the twin uses them.

Will it send emails or book things for me?

No. Calendar and email are off by default. When you connect them, Heirloom can read and draft, but it never sends or books anything on its own.

Can I clone someone else's voice?

Only with their clear permission. Heirloom is for preserving your own voice, or a family member's who has agreed to take part.

Installing and hardware

What computer do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) PC with about 40 GB of free disk space. A recent NVIDIA or AMD graphics card makes it faster; without one it needs 16 GB of RAM or more. See the hardware tiers.

Is it hard to install?

No. It's one Windows installer that needs no admin rights, no WSL and no reboot. It checks your hardware and sets up the model, voice and speech recognition for you. Running it again later is safe and keeps your data.

Does it work on a Mac?

Not yet. Heirloom runs on Windows PCs and on the Twin Box appliance today.

Why does Windows show a warning when I run the installer?

During the beta the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm. We'll sign it before the public launch.

What is the Twin Box?

An optional dedicated home appliance with its own graphics card that runs Heirloom around the clock for the whole family, so your own PC stays free.

Family and phones

How does my family use it?

You name heirs and family members, and they get their own access to talk with your twin, now or later. You can also seal letters that open on a date, an age, or a day you choose.

Is there a phone app?

Yes, for Android and iPhone, in beta. It pairs with your Heirloom by scanning a QR code, then lets you chat or talk with the twin from your phone.

Can family call the twin on the phone?

Optionally. With your own Twilio phone number, family can call or text the twin. The carrier only carries the call; listening, thinking and speaking happen at home, and every call starts by saying it's an AI. It's off unless you set it up.
