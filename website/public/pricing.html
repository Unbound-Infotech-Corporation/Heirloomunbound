Pricing questions

Why isn't it a monthly subscription?

Cloud AI apps charge monthly because every message costs them server time. Heirloom runs on your hardware, so you own it outright.

Do I need to buy new hardware?

Usually not. Heirloom runs on most Windows 10 and 11 PCs, faster with a recent graphics card. See the hardware tiers.

Are there any ongoing costs?

Only if you choose the optional phone line, which uses your own Twilio account and is billed by Twilio. Everything else runs locally.

What is founding-family pricing?

Families who join the beta and help us test get a lower launch price. We'll confirm the details by email before the launch.
