Heirloom Unbound · Privacy

Local models downloaded during Setup stay on this computer. The vault
(your stories) lives in the folder you choose — Documents\HeirloomVault
by default, or a second disk on a Dedicated PC.

Heirloom Unbound does not upload local weights. Cloud vendors
(ElevenLabs, D-ID, fal) are optional and only receive what you paste
after you create those accounts yourself.

A Dedicated PC may start with Windows and warm local engines. Live room
listen defaults toward on for that role and remains togglable.

Unsigned Setup.exe: Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run. Choose
More info → Run anyway. Reputation accumulates with installs; we do not
pretend this build is code-signed.
