START HERE — Heirloom for your computer
=======================================

This is the whole Heirloom app: your twin, look at the screen, mail,
and Unbound Keyboard. Not just the writing card.

1. Unzip this. Put the folder on your Desktop.
   Do not double-click anything from inside the zip.
2. Open the folder.
3. Double-click  Heirloom.bat
   If you only see a Windows folder, open that first, then
   double-click Heirloom.bat.
4. First time takes about a minute. Leave the window open.
   If Windows shows a blue box: More info → Run anyway.
5. Tap Continue with Google. A browser opens. Sign in there.
   Heirloom never sees that password. Then talk to your twin
   in the big window.

Unbound Keyboard is still here: tray → Unbound Keyboard, or
Ctrl+Shift+U. Or double-click Try-Unbound-Keyboard.bat if you
only want the writing card.

When the writing card opens, type:

     I recieve this and should of said thanks just just just

Click Fix spelling, or tap a yellow chip. Leave it if you meant
what you typed. Then try Put this where I was typing.

This try-it copy may not be signed in yet. Spelling still works
without signing in. Sign in with Google in the big window (or on
the writing card). A slip is a Heirloom email after you tap
Send a sign-in note. Look for “Your Unbound Keyboard sign-in slip”.
It starts with ml_. Check spam. If nothing arrives, skip that note
and use Continue with Google instead. The website box is the
Heirloom page in your browser (https://…) — not a street address.
We never ask for a Google, Microsoft, Windows, or phone password.

MAC
---
Double-click run.sh in this folder (or in the Windows folder).

ANDROID PHONE
-------------
The phone keyboard is a separate file: UnboundKeyboard.apk
Copy it to the phone and tap it. Then Settings → System →
Languages & input → On-screen keyboard → Unbound Keyboard.

IPHONE
------
Apple does not let us install this keyboard. Use Write inside
the Heirloom app instead.
